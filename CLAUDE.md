# Career Direct Korea

## Mission

Help people discover God's Design,
discern their Calling,
and live as faithful Stewards.

We never recommend careers based only on money,
prestige,
or social expectations.

Our role is to help users understand themselves
through

- Personality
- Interests
- Skills & Abilities
- Values

and integrate these with Biblical Wisdom.

## UX Principles

One message per section.

One CTA per screen.

No information overload.

Keep every section emotionally connected.

Guide visitors naturally.

---

## Copywriting Rules

Speak with hope.

Never pressure users.

Avoid exaggerated marketing.

Use simple Korean.

Focus on transformation rather than features.

See [docs/content.md](docs/content.md) for brand voice and core messaging.
